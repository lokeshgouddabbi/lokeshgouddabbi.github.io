Vision

Regenerative farming systems for a sustainable future

My vision is regenerative farming systems that work for soil, climate, and people together. I want to spend my career at the intersection of research, policy-making, and diplomacy - using evidence from soil and climate science to shape agricultural and land-use policy, and carrying that evidence from the field into international engagement, delegations, and institutions.

At its core, this is about service: serving people and nation through research, policy engagement, and the kind of delegated, diplomatic work that turns shared visions for a sustainable future into practice.

About

From greenhouse trials to COP negotiating rooms

I'm an M.Sc. candidate in Organic Agriculture and Food Systems at the University of Hohenheim, after a B.Sc. (Hons) in Agriculture from Amity University and certificate coursework in organic farming through IIT Kharagpur, IGNOU, ICAR–NAARM, and PJTSAU Hyderabad.

Across research posts at the Thünen Institute, the LUFA laboratory in Speyer, and Hohenheim, my work has moved between soil-chemistry benches and continent-scale land-cover data. My most recent projects - on soil carbon and biodiversity in intensively farmed landscapes - sit right at the center of that path: how farming choices show up in soil carbon and biodiversity outcomes, and how that evidence can inform EU land-use and climate policy, from LULUCF accounting to Land Degradation Neutrality.
